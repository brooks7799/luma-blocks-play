Luma Blocks — Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026. Applies to the Luma Blocks app on iOS, Android and the web. Operated by Create Magic, LLC (hello@createmagic.co).

The short version

Luma Blocks has no account and never asks who you are. On first launch the game asks whether it may collect crash reports and anonymous gameplay statistics. If you say no, nothing described under “Optional data” is collected. You can change your answer at any time in Settings ▸ More Settings ▸ Data Sharing.

Data that stays on your device

Your best score, unlocked themes, sound and haptic preferences, your consent answer and your Remove Ads entitlement are stored locally on the device (or in your browser for the web version). We cannot see them.

Optional data (only after you say yes)

WhatWho processes itWhy
Crash reports: device model, OS version, app version, the state of the app when it crashed, a random installation idFirebase Crashlytics (Google)Finding and fixing crashes

None of this includes your name, email, contacts, precise location or the contents of your device. Events are aggregated per run, never per move.

Advertising

The iOS and Android versions show interstitial ads between runs and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to continue a run. Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay mediation with Unity Ads as the ad network. To serve and measure ads these partners receive your IP address, device and OS information, and — only if you allow it — the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, via the App Tracking Transparency prompt; Advertising ID on Android, which you can reset or delete in system settings). Denying tracking still shows ads, just not personalised ones. Their policies: Unity. The web version shows no ads.

A one-time Remove Ads Forever purchase removes interstitial ads permanently. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we receive a receipt that contains no payment details.

Children

Luma Blocks is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, email us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

Crash and analytics data is retained by the processors above for up to 14 months and then deleted or fully aggregated. Because the only identifier is a random installation id, deleting the app (or clearing site data in your browser) severs the link between you and any data already sent. To request deletion of data tied to an installation id, email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your data, to object, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority. We do not sell personal data. Withdraw consent in the app’s settings or by email; we answer within 30 days.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when anything changes, and prompt for consent again in the app if the change is material.
