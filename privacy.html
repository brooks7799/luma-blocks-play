Luma Blocks — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026. Applies to the Luma Blocks app on iOS, Android and the web. Operated by Create Magic, LLC (hello@createmagic.co).

The short version

Luma Blocks has no account and never asks who you are. Crash reports and anonymous gameplay statistics are collected by default, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings ▸ More Settings ▸ Data Sharing. Turning it off stops everything described under “Optional data”.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game asks before collecting anything: nothing under “Optional data” is collected there until you say yes, and saying no is a normal answer that changes nothing else about the game.

Advertising is separate from all of that. The iOS and Android versions show ads either way, and saying no to data sharing does not turn them off. What ads can use about you is controlled by your system settings instead — App Tracking Transparency on iOS, and the Android ads settings on Android. Because we do not treat the data-sharing setting or the EEA/UK consent prompt as GDPR consent for advertising, ads are requested as non-personalised.

Data that stays on your device

Your best score, unlocked themes, sound and haptic preferences, your data-sharing choice and your Remove Ads entitlement are stored locally on the device (or in your browser for the web version). We cannot see them.

Identifiers

Analytics and crash reporting use a per-install identifier rather than anything tied to you as a person: on iOS this is the vendor identifier (IDFV), and on Android and the web it is a random id generated at first launch. Deleting the app (or clearing site data) ends that identifier.

The advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) is used only for advertising, and only where your system settings allow it. It is resettable — and on Android deletable — in your device settings, and denying App Tracking Transparency on iOS stops it being read at all.

Advertising

The iOS and Android versions show interstitial ads between runs and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to continue a run. Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay mediation with Unity Ads as the ad network. To serve and measure ads these partners receive your IP address, device and OS information, and — only if you allow it — the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, via the App Tracking Transparency prompt; Advertising ID on Android, which you can reset or delete in system settings). Denying tracking still shows ads, just not personalised ones. Their policies: Unity. The web version shows no ads.

A one-time Remove Ads Forever purchase removes interstitial ads permanently. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we receive a receipt that contains no payment details. While data sharing is on, the fact that a purchase happened is recorded as one anonymous analytics event — no payment details, no receipt contents, and nothing that identifies you.

Children

Luma Blocks is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, email us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days. Gameplay events are kept by GameAnalytics for up to 12 months, with detail progressively reduced after the first three months, and by Firebase Analytics for 2 months. After those periods the data is deleted or retained only in aggregate form that cannot be traced to a device.

Because the only identifier is a per-install identifier, deleting the app (or clearing site data in your browser) severs the link between you and any data already sent. To request deletion of data tied to an installation id, email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your data, to object, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority.

We do not sell personal data for money. However, where you allow tracking, the device advertising identifier may be shared with our advertising partners in a way that California law treats as a “sale” or a “share” for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out by denying App Tracking Transparency on iOS, by deleting the advertising ID in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads on Android, or by emailing us. Analytics and crash reporting are separate from that and can be switched off at any time in Settings ▸ More Settings ▸ Data Sharing. We answer requests within 30 days.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when anything changes, and ask again in the app — in the EEA and UK, where consent is what we rely on — if the change is material.
